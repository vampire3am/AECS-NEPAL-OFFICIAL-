# AECS visual system

## Direction

Minimal, aesthetic and professional as explicitly requested after reviewing the editorial AECS concept. Bright daylight use on phones and laptops calls for light, high-contrast surfaces. The homepage is Persuade; destination guides are Read; planning, booking and staff pages are Operate. Build directly in code from the confirmed direction; no image comp is being treated as an approved design.

## Palette

White #ffffff, cool paper #f5f7fa, ink #10273f, muted #536173, navy #102c46, action orange #e77624, accessible orange text #a3470b, border #dce3e9. Orange buttons use deep ink text. Green and red reserved for real statuses.

## Typography

Self-hosted Manrope for display and body, 400–800 weights, body 16px minimum, labels 14px minimum. Restrained tracking down to -0.035em. Editorial display headings balanced and below 6rem.

## Composition

Wide gutters, 1200px content measure; strong asymmetry in the opening and photo-led destination stories. Small corner radii, fine dividers, no nested cards, no gradients or decorative badges. White/navy sections supply rhythm. No eyebrow labels above headings.

## Interaction

Destination selection updates an accessible comparison tray. Forms have explicit pending, validation, saved and failed states. Navigation reflows to a complete mobile menu. Focus rings, reduced motion and semantic HTML are mandatory.
